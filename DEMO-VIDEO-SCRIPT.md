# Demo video script — Telegram Glance (≤ 3 minutes)

Record in the Even Hub simulator (or the browser preview at the GitHub
Pages demo). Total target: ~2 min 30 s. Speak Bahasa Indonesia or English;
keep the glasses canvas visible the whole time.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem (show phone buzzing / Telegram on desktop)

> "Telegram keeps buzzing, but on smart glasses you can't type or scroll a
> feed. Telegram Glance puts the one message that matters on your Even G2 —
> and lets you answer it with one gesture."

## 0:20–0:50 — Home glance (simulator canvas)

- Show the Home screen: 2 unread chats, last-message previews, live
  BTC/ETH/SOL line from CoinGecko.
- "Three unread, two chats, prices live — one glance, no phone."

## 0:50–1:30 — Read + AI draft + send (the read-and-write moment)

- Tap to select "Dev Group", swipe down to open it.
- Show the AI draft under the messages; tap to cycle through the 3 drafts.
- Double-tap to open the Draft screen, double-tap again to send.
- Show the Sent confirmation. "Read it, picked a draft, sent — three
  gestures, no typing."

## 1:30–2:00 — Voice reply

- Swipe down from Chat to the Voice screen, hold to record, speak
  "Yes, shipping tonight", release to stop, double-tap to send.
- (In the browser preview, type the sentence in the voice-simulation box —
  say so on camera: "voice is simulated here; on G2 this is the mic".)

## 2:00–2:25 — Crypto glance (Track 2)

- Swipe up from Home to the Crypto screen: live BTC/ETH/SOL + 24h
  change, read-only. Tap to refresh.
- "The same glance flow covers the prices your crypto groups talk about —
  read-only, no trading, no funds."

## 2:25–2:35 — Close

- Double-tap on Home shows the exit dialog.
- "Telegram Glance — Track 1 Messaging & News, Track 2 Crypto & DeFi.
  Built for Even G2 during the Heads Up hackathon. Repo link in the
  description."

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Simulator at http://localhost:5173 (or Pages demo URL)
- [ ] Backend running? Optional — DEMO label visible is fine and honest
- [ ] Canvas readable at 1080p (crop to the simulator window)
- [ ] No API keys / tokens visible on screen at any point
- [ ] Final cut ≤ 3:00, export 1080p
